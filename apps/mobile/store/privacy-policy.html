VoltAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Applies to the VoltAI mobile app (Android and iOS), bundle identifier uz.voltai.app. The same policy is published at voltai.uz/uz/privacy.

VoltAI is a map of electric-vehicle (EV) charging stations in Uzbekistan, with a trip planner that works out where to stop and charge. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version: VoltAI has no user accounts and asks you to sign in to nothing. It does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your location is used on your device to show the map around you. The one time information leaves your phone is when you plan a trip: the start and end points you chose and your car’s figures are sent to VoltAI’s server to compute the route, with no name, account or device identifier attached. Everything you configure — preferences, cars, saved trips — stays on your phone.

Information the app uses

  • Location (foreground only). With your permission, VoltAI reads your device’s location while you are actively using the app (“When In Use”). On the device it centres the map on you and shows which chargers are closest. VoltAI does not access your location in the background and does not track your movements. Your current position is not sent to VoltAI’s servers by the map. You can decline or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings; the map still works, you just will not see your own position on it.
  • The map itself. The map is drawn by Yandex MapKit, a mapping component made by Yandex. Like any online map, it fetches map tiles and imagery from Yandex’s servers as you move around, and when the “my location” layer is on it processes your device location to draw the blue dot. That processing is done by the Yandex component under Yandex’s own terms and privacy policy; VoltAI does not receive or store it.
  • Trip planning. When you ask for a route, the app sends to VoltAI’s server the start and destination coordinates you selected (either a place you picked on the map, a saved destination, or your current position if you chose “from here”), together with the figures of the car you are planning with: its real-world range, connector type, maximum charging speed, energy consumption, charging-curve preset, starting charge level, and your driving-style and temperature choices. Our server uses these to compute charging stops. To obtain the road geometry it forwards the start and end coordinates (only those) to a third-party routing provider, MyTaxi. No name, email, phone number, account, advertising ID or device identifier accompanies any of this — the request is anonymous. The coordinates you choose are used only to compute that one route and are not stored on our server. Only routes between the app’s built-in list of Uzbek cities — which are fixed places in the app, not your location — are cached on the server so the same intercity route need not be recomputed; a route that involves a point you picked or your current position is held in temporary memory only and is never written to storage.
  • Your preferences, cars and saved trips. The theme you choose, the cars in your garage (name, range, connector, charging details) and the trips you have planned are saved only on your device using local on-device storage. They are not associated with any account or identifier and are never uploaded — except that, as described above, a car’s figures are included in a plan request when you plan a trip with it.
  • Standard connection data. When the app requests station data, statuses, a route or the app’s configuration, our server (and the network edge in front of it) sees the ordinary technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address and the app’s version. We use this only to serve the request and keep the service running; we do not build profiles from it.

Information the app does NOT collect

  • No accounts. There is no registration, login, email, phone number or password. We cannot identify you because we never ask who you are.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. VoltAI includes no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking or profiling tools. We do not build a profile of you or follow you across apps or websites.
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking.
  • No sale of personal data. We do not sell or rent information about you to anyone. The only sharing that happens is the routing request described above, which contains no personal identifiers.

Where the station data comes from

The charging-station locations, connector and power details, prices and live availability shown in VoltAI are aggregated from the public mobile apps of several EV charging operators active in Uzbekistan and presented together on one map. This is information about public charging infrastructure — where stations are and whether a plug is free — not information about you. VoltAI is an independent app: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any charging operator, and the data is unofficial. It is refreshed every few minutes but may be delayed, incomplete or occasionally wrong; always check the charger itself before relying on it. When the app displays availability, your device requests station data from VoltAI’s service over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection; these requests carry no personal identifiers.

On-device storage and retention

Because there are no accounts and no server-side user records, the only data VoltAI retains about you lives on your own device. It stays there until you clear it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or by uninstalling the app, which deletes all locally stored preferences, cars, saved trips and the offline copy of the station list. On our side there is nothing to delete: plan requests are not stored against any person, and the coordinates you choose are never written to storage — only routes between the app’s built-in cities are cached, and those are fixed places, not your location.

Children’s privacy

VoltAI is intended for a general audience of drivers and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Network requests between the app and VoltAI’s service use standard encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS). Since VoltAI does not operate user accounts, there is no personal database that could be exposed in a breach. Information stored on your device is protected by your device’s own operating-system protections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or in legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated version at the same URL. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
