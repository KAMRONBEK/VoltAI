## Full description

VoltAI is the fastest way to find an electric-vehicle charger in Uzbekistan. It brings every major charging operator together on a single live map, so you can see at a glance where the nearest stations are, what they support, and whether a plug is free right now — without hopping between half a dozen separate operator apps.

Each station shows a real-time availability dot, so you know before you drive whether a connector is open, in use, or offline. Tap any station for its address, connector types, and power output, and get directions in one tap. Powerful filters let you narrow the map to exactly what your car needs: filter by connector type, by minimum charging power, by operator, by city, or show only stations that are available right now.

VoltAI is account-free and private by design. There is no sign-up, no login, and no password — open the app and the map is there. It uses your location only while the app is open, purely to show nearby chargers and estimate distance; that never leaves your device. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Any preferences or vehicle details you enter stay on your phone.

Built for real driving in Uzbekistan, VoltAI keeps the map fast and readable, remembers your preferences on-device, and focuses on one job: getting you to a working charger. Whether you drive daily or you're planning a longer trip across the country, VoltAI shows you the whole charging network in one place.
